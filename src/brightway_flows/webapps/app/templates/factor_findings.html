Where the two numbers come from

A publisher files one substance twice in one compartment, under two names. EF 3.1 ships (Z)-HFC-1336 and (2z)-1,1,1,4,4,4-hexafluorobut-2-ene as separate rows in the same air compartment; every field this pipeline compares matches, so it holds them to be one flow, keeps one and deprecates the other. Both rows carried a characterisation factor, and the two factors did not agree.

Nothing here was changed by this list. The number kept is the more precise of the two where they are one number written twice, and the survivor's otherwise; the other is recorded rather than dropped, which is what this page shows.
