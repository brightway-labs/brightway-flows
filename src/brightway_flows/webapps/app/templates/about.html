About

The Brightway Flows is built and maintained by Brightway Labs, and published openly for the Brightway community and for anyone else who works with life cycle inventory data.

Why we built it

Every inventory database names its substances differently, and nothing in those names says whether two entries mean the same thing. We built this list so that a flow in one database can be translated to its counterpart in another, with every step of the translation open to inspection. Why this exists

Contribute or report a problem

If a flow is matched to the wrong substance, published under the wrong name, filed in the wrong compartment or given a factor that cannot be its own, tell us. Every flow and substance page has links that open a pre-filled issue, and you do not need to run a build or clone anything: name the row and say what your evidence is, and the measurement is done for you.

The list also has questions of its own waiting on a person — one registry number claimed by two names, a rename awaiting a ruling. Those are at the queues, and answering one takes a verdict and a paragraph saying why.
