# Changes to BAFU 2026 v1

*Part of [What was changed in each source](index.md). What is still open about BAFU is on the issue tracker under the label [`bafu`](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Abafu).*

BAFU 2026 v1 arrives as an ecoSpold v1 archive, from which 2,679 elementary
flows are extracted, and it is merged third — after ecoinvent 3.12 and
ecoinvent 3.8 — on the build of 2026-08-29 from commit `54b0f9d`. It ships
no identifier of its own: a row is a name, a compartment, a sub-compartment
and a unit, and the same substance is written once per sub-compartment. The
uuids this project gives BAFU rows are derived from those four fields (and a
country suffix where one is present), so this page identifies every row by
name, original compartment and unit rather than by uuid.

Corrections to what BAFU shipped are in `bafu-2026-v1-manual-fixes.json`
(71 entries: 47 registry numbers, 9 synonyms, 7 units, 6 names, 2
compartments) and, because BAFU inherits ecoinvent 2's spellings and habits
through SimaPro, in `simapro-lineage-manual-fixes.json` (8 entries, applied
to BAFU and to Stepwise 2006 alike). Where a row belongs is stated by hand
for 29 rows in `bafu-2026-v1-match-overrides.json`. On the build, 2,564 rows
matched by rule, 23 by a curated target, and 92 became new substances.

## One name, two substances

On the 2026-08-29 build of `54b0f9d` five BAFU names ended up on two
different substances. Two of those were defects, and both were corrected the
next day ([#177](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues/177), [#183](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues/183)); on the build of 2026-08-30 at `9fbb0d5` three names
remain split, and in each the split is forced by BAFU's own data — a registry
number on one row that the others lack, or a compartment that changes what the
substance physically is. All five are described here, the two corrected ones
in the past tense, so that a reader of either build finds the row.

**`Aluminium`** — BAFU writes the name on 14 rows. The one in
`emissions to water / unspecified` carries `22537-23-1`, which is the
aluminium *ion*'s number, so it is published as `Aluminium(3+)`. The
`resources / in ground` row carries `7429-90-5`, the metal's number, and the
other twelve carry none and are matched on the name; all thirteen are
`Aluminium`. The number outranks the name ([When a name and a CAS number
disagree](../deciding/identity.md)), and it was BAFU's own CAS number.

| BAFU name | Compartment | Unit | BAFU's CAS | Published as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aluminium | emissions to water / unspecified | kg | 22537-23-1 | Aluminium(3+) |
| Aluminium | resources / in ground | kg | 7429-90-5 | Aluminium |
| Aluminium | 12 other emission compartments (air ×4, soil ×3, water ×5) | kg | — | Aluminium |

**`Manganese`** — the same shape. The `emissions to air / unspecified` row
carries `16397-91-4`, the manganese(2+) ion's number, and is published as
`Manganese(2+)`; the other thirteen rows carry the metal's `7439-96-5` or
nothing, and are `Manganese`.

| BAFU name | Compartment | Unit | BAFU's CAS | Published as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manganese | emissions to air / unspecified | kg | 16397-91-4 | Manganese(2+) |
| Manganese | 6 water compartments, resources / in ground | kg | 7439-96-5 | Manganese |
| Manganese | air high. pop., low. pop., low. pop. long-term; soil ×3 | kg | — | Manganese |

**`Copper ion`** — three rows, and a split that has been corrected. The one
in `emissions to air / high. pop.` carried `7440-50-8`, which is the CAS number
of copper the *metal*, and on the 2026-08-29 build it landed on `Copper`
beside BAFU's own plain `Copper` row in that compartment, while the two rows
without a CAS number — `emissions to air / unspecified` and `emissions to
water / unspecified` — were matched on the name and landed on `Copper, Ion`,
the substance for copper dissolved with its charge unstated. The metal's
number is BAFU's own: the archive writes it on all 38 exchanges of that row.
The name is the half that is right — BAFU already lists plain `Copper` under
7440-50-8 in this very compartment — so the number is removed from the row in
`bafu-2026-v1-manual-fixes.json`, and on the build of 2026-08-30 at `9fbb0d5` all three
`Copper ion` rows land on `Copper, Ion` ([#183](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues/183)). Worth reporting to BAFU.

**`Water`** — 169 rows, more than any other BAFU name (the count includes
the country-suffixed copies described under *Rows merged into one flow*).
Water released to air is water vapour, and the 55 rows in the four air
sub-compartments are published on `Water vapour`; the 114 rows released to
water — river, ocean, groundwater, fossil water and unspecified — are
published on `Water`. Same name, two physical states, and the compartment is
what says which.

**`Carbon monoxide, biogenic`** — three rows, and a defect that has been
corrected. The `high. pop.` and `unspecified` air rows carry `630-08-0` and
land on `Carbon Monoxide (biogenic)`, the substance EF 3.1 characterises.
The `low. pop.` row ships with no CAS number at all — none of the 117
exchanges of it in BAFU's archive writes one — so on the 2026-08-29 build its
name alone reached no existing substance and it minted a second one, `Carbon
Monoxide, Biogenic`, with one flow and no characterisation factors. It was
the same pattern `Carbon dioxide, biogenic` had until its missing number was
supplied, and it got the same fix: 630-08-0 is written on the name in
`bafu-2026-v1-manual-fixes.json`, and on the build of 2026-08-30 at `9fbb0d5` all three
rows land together ([#177](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues/177)).

| BAFU name | Compartment | Unit | BAFU's CAS | Published as, 2026-08-29 | Published as, 2026-08-30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon monoxide, biogenic | emissions to air / high. pop. | kg | 630-08-0 | Carbon Monoxide (biogenic) | Carbon Monoxide (biogenic) |
| Carbon monoxide, biogenic | emissions to air / unspecified | kg | 630-08-0 | Carbon Monoxide (biogenic) | Carbon Monoxide (biogenic) |
| Carbon monoxide, biogenic | emissions to air / low. pop. | kg | — (630-08-0 supplied) | Carbon Monoxide, Biogenic (a new substance) | Carbon Monoxide (biogenic) |

One more correction belongs beside these, although no BAFU name was split by
it: BAFU's five `Hydrocarbons, unspecified` rows (`emissions to air /
unspecified`; `emissions to water /` unspecified, ocean, lake and river) carry
no number and differ from EF 3.1's `hydrocarbons (unspecified)` by a comma
and a pair of brackets, and on the 2026-08-29 build they landed on a
duplicate bucket two ecoinvent rows had minted. Five curated targets in
`bafu-2026-v1-match-overrides.json` now send them to EF's own substance; the
lake and river rows keep their water body, which EF does not have, and the
bucket gets a flow there ([#178](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues/178)).

## An ionic charge was added or corrected

BAFU writes a charge as a roman numeral — `Chromium VI`, `Cadmium II`,
`Tin (II)` — and the list writes the same charge as `Chromium(6+)`,
`Cadmium(2+)`, `Tin(2+)`. That is notation, and on the build it covers 11
names and 26 rows; none of it is listed here. Three names did gain a charge
their spelling did not state.

| BAFU name | Compartment | Unit | Published as | Where the charge came from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aluminium | emissions to water / unspecified | kg | Aluminium(3+) | BAFU's own CAS number on the row, 22537-23-1, is the Al³⁺ ion's |
| Manganese | emissions to air / unspecified | kg | Manganese(2+) | BAFU's own CAS number on the row, 16397-91-4, is the Mn²⁺ ion's |
| Copper ion | emissions to air / high. pop., / unspecified; emissions to water / unspecified | kg | Copper, Ion | No charge: the rows state none and no CAS number says one, so the list publishes the generic ion rather than guess. The high-population row joined the other two once the metal's number was taken off it ([#183](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues/183)) |

Two names lose the word `ion` on publication without any change of meaning:
`Ammonium, ion` (six water rows) is published as `Ammonium` and
`Perchlorate, ion` (one river row) as `Perchlorate`, because ammonium and
perchlorate are ions by definition and the word adds nothing.

Six of BAFU's roman-numeral rows shipped with the wrong number or none, and
their charge was *confirmed* rather than added by writing the ion's number
on the row; those are under *Registry numbers corrected* below.

## Rows merged into one flow

On the build, 237 consensus flows carry more than one BAFU row. They fall
into a small number of patterns.

### Country suffixes

BAFU appends a country or region code to the name of a flow when a dataset
reports it for a place: `Water, AT`, `Water, FR`, `Water, unspecified
natural origin, CN`. The suffix says where the water was used, which the
list records nowhere in a flow — a flow is a substance and a compartment —
so the suffix is stripped and the row lands with its unsuffixed sibling. 261
rows carry a suffix, and 87 distinct codes are seen: the ISO-2 countries
from `AE` to `ZA`, and the regions `Europe`, `GLO`, `OECD`, `RAF` and
`RER`. The names it happens to:

| Water, unspecified natural origin | 78 | Water, Resource → Water |
| Water, cooling, unspecified natural origin | 7 | Cooling water |
| Water, embodied in product | 5 | Water |
| Water, well | 4 | Groundwater |
| Water, river | 3 | River water |
| Water, lake | 1 | Lake water |

The single kilogram row in each water compartment was converted to cubic
metres at 1 kg = 0.001 m³ (`bafu-2026-v1-manual-fixes.json`,
[#78](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues/78)), so
that it lands beside its 53 suffixed siblings rather than on a flow of its
own; the air rows stay in kilograms, which is how EF 3.1 states water vapour.

### Resource corrections

BAFU carries eleven flows whose name ends `, resource correction`. A dataset
built with recycled material books a *negative* amount of the primary
resource charged upstream — BAFU's own note on one dataset reads "Resource
correction added for share of primary aluminium" — so the correction and the
extraction are one quantity stated in two places, and only their sum is the
extraction that happened. Nine of the eleven carry the base substance's own
registry number and land with it on that alone; `Gravel` and `Energy, gross
calorific value, in biomass` carry none and were given the base name as a
synonym so that they land the same way
([#69](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues/69)).

### Energy carriers by mass and by energy

BAFU ships each fossil and nuclear fuel twice: as a mass (`Coal, hard`, kg)
and as an energy content (`Energy, from coal`, MJ). EF 3.1 accounts these
resources by energy, so both rows land on the MJ flow, the energy row as it
is and the mass row with a stated heating value. Measured over all 11,947
datasets in the release, the mass row and the energy row of a fuel never
appear in the same dataset — `Energy, from coal` in 14 datasets, `Coal,
hard` in 139, none carrying both — so nothing is counted twice by sharing a
flow ([#3](https://github.com/brightway-labs/brightway-flows/issues/3)).
All are `resources / in ground` unless stated.

### The other corrections in the fix files

**Names.** `Energy, waste heat, air` (`emissions to air / unspecified`, MJ)
is renamed `Heat, waste`, BAFU's own name for the same release on fourteen
other rows. `Gas, natural/m3` and `Gas, mine, off-gas, process, coal
mining/m3`, both with the unit field reading `Nm3`, lose the `/m3` from the
name: the unit field is believed. `Wood, unspecified, standing/kg` and
`Water, process, unspecified natural origin/kg` lose their suffix after the
unit conversions below, and `Water, process, unspecified natural origin/m3`
in `resources / in water` becomes `Water, unspecified natural origin`, which
is BAFU's own name for the same water on 78 other rows. From the lineage
file, `Uranium, 451 GJ per kg`, `Uranium, 560 GJ per kg` and `Uranium, 2291
GJ per kg` become `Uranium`, and `Carbon dioxide, in air` with `124-38-9`
becomes `Carbon dioxide, biogenic, in air`, so that the CAS number is read
beside an origin. Every renamed row keeps its shipped spelling as a synonym
for matching.

**Units.** Seven kg rows of liquid water become m³ at 1 kg = 0.001 m³:
`Water` (four water compartments), `Waste water` (ocean, river),
`Chemically polluted water` (river), `Water, process, surface`, `Water,
embodied in product` and `Water, process, unspecified natural origin` (three
compartments). `Wood, unspecified, standing/kg` becomes m³ at 0.00204 m³/kg.
The three `Uranium, N GJ per kg` rows become MJ at the factor their own name
states — 451,000, 560,000 and 2,291,000 MJ/kg.

**Compartments.** The two `Carbon dioxide, in air` rows filed under
`resources / biotic` and `resources / unspecified` move to `resources / in
air`: the name says where the carbon comes from and the compartment
contradicted it.

**Synonyms.** `COD, Chemical Oxygen Demand`, `BOD5, Biological Oxygen
Demand` and `TOC, Total Organic Carbon` are given EF 3.1's shorter names as
synonyms so that they land on EF's flows, exactly as ecoinvent's identical
strings already do through its correspondence table; `Energy, gross
calorific value, in biomass` and its resource correction are given
`Biomass`, and `Gravel, resource correction` is given `Gravel`, for the
reasons above.
