The mine-gas case illustrates why the earlier descriptions need a dated
recovery record. Its summary said that the raw-volume row still needed an
original observation. The complete source export contains 99 such observations,
and all 99 match their original XML exchanges. The 12 standard-volume
observations also match. The interface now marks the earlier statement as
superseded and preserves it for traceability. Gas reference temperature,
pressure and composition remain unestablished.

This follow-up covers the 39 descriptions previously marked partially
supported. Each case retains its original summary, source references and first
comparison. A dated recovery record names what was received, the exact original
claim it answers, the supporting source hashes and the remaining questions.
Two of the original 39 descriptions are now supported within their stated
lookup/metadata scope; 36 remain partially supported. One description conflicts
with the recovered evidence and is explicitly superseded. The five previously
supported descriptions remain unchanged. No recovery creates scientific
sign-off, advances a lifecycle stage or applies a correction.

same-input experiment. The adoption-diff report carries a 22 September run;
