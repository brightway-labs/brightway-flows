# Can Consensus Flows receive the Workbench biosphere work?

For the two titanium-dioxide resource flows, the upstream correspondence retains
the titanium fraction **0.599**, yet the inspected current native EF mineral
calculation applies it **zero times**. The new consumer can compose that
conversion and hold an unsupported application instead of silently producing a
factor. That is a useful upgrade, but it is also evidence that retaining a mapping
and a provider number does not establish a correct product result.

**The integrated design is a suitable foundation. It has not yet demonstrated a
complete replacement of the Workbench's biosphere alignment and consumers.**
Much of the original work is preserved. Original input qualification, executable
policy migration and receiving the resulting consumer releases remain incomplete.
The historical “zero CF conflicts” and “all sixteen categories for every product”
statements need narrower, dated evidence.

This assessment inspects real retained records, source code and the live
[published Workbench biosphere page](https://lci-workbench-0d71e7.gitlab.io/#/biosphere).
It introduces no example findings, synthetic scientific results, approval or
inventory changes. Source-only candidates and test fixtures remain distinct.

## What the published page actually establishes

The live capture contains the HTML and application assets, all three biosphere
panels and all sixteen BAFU EF method-detail JSON files. Each response has its
URL, retrieval time, status, size and SHA-256 in the local capture manifest.

1. **Several generations share one page.** The
   [summary](https://lci-workbench-0d71e7.gitlab.io/data/biosphere-summary.json)
   names a September 4 export, embeds the August 24 mapping, and records an
   October 10 refresh from committed files. The
   [consensus panel](https://lci-workbench-0d71e7.gitlab.io/data/biosphere-consensus.json)
   separately names run `20260922T2037421996740000`. The refresh explicitly
   does not read a live LCA project; its health components remain older evidence.
2. **The zero-conflict headline has a small, specific denominator.** The live
   `cf_validation_summary` reports five validated comparisons and zero
   discrepancies. The inspected exporter compares climate factors by lowercased
   name with 5% tolerance; it does not compare every substance, compartment,
   quantity basis and method. The older health block reports 182 of 193 within
   tolerance. Neither establishes universally conflict-free alignment. The
   original broader before/after reports record 4,703/12,445 and 7,777/16,558
   conflicts/comparisons; their differing populations prevent treating them as
   a controlled before/after experiment.
3. **Sixteen method tables exist.** This audit received every corresponding
   published detail. The exporter sets BAFU-keyed method coverage to 100%
   because those CFs already use BAFU keys. That denominator is the existing
   CF table, not every required flow in every product. Detail tables are bounded
   views, often limited to the largest hundred factors. Neither a table's
   presence nor a successfully computed score proves scientific completeness.
4. **The Agribalyse claims need reconciliation.** The page reports 1,615 passes
   out of 1,617, while the embedded verification block says zero failures and
   a separate action item says two failures. One section says 287 uncharacterised
   flows and the methodology says 505. The original placeholder input also has
   missing-identifier and overwritten-row problems described in the source
   audit. A missing CF must remain a missing CF; it cannot automatically mean
   “safe to ignore.” The page also mixes 3.2 prose with a 4.0 placeholder registry.
5. **Source roles and authorship need correction.** The
   [source panel](https://lci-workbench-0d71e7.gitlab.io/data/biosphere-sources.json)
   counts eight builder external inputs, seven contributing. That differs from
   the requested eight source families and from the ten mechanism labels used
   by the UI's source counter. GLAD, DDS and Sentier links have dependencies.
   All 95 inspected HESTIA mapping rows name **Eaternity** as mapper and leave
   verifier blank; the Oxford vocabulary's authorship does not establish an
   independent Oxford-verified BAFU correspondence.

## How to receive everything without repeating the old architecture

Keep this project's consensus substance and context decisions at the centre. Receive
GLAD, DDS, FEDEFL, HESTIA and partner mappings as **attributed source assertions**
with versioned evidence and an explicit disposition. A source assertion can
corroborate, disagree, remain unresolved or propose a change; correlated bridges
cannot become extra independent votes. Agribalyse 3.2 and locally retained 4.0
material require separate source identities and qualification.

Every historical record needs one of four destinations: a tested upstream
correction, a retained consumer policy, historical evidence with a successor,
or explicit retirement with a reason. Preserve the original row in all four
cases. The current Curation case/module relationship can express the review
work. The actual source rows belong in versioned source inputs; the executable
decision belongs in the existing ruling or transformer register.

Connect each numerical result to a retained chain:
**original observation → source/version → consensus decision → quantity binding
→ method implementation and selected policy → generated factors → validation
→ accepted consumer bundle → registered and served release**.
An unresolved link remains visible and prevents the corresponding completion
claim. Keep checks scoped to their actual population and distinguish explicit
zero, missing factor, unknown identity, unsupported method and held policy.

### Captured evidence for each proposed mapping

For example, the titanium decision must cite the original TiO₂ observation,
the exact upstream correspondence carrying 0.599, the target titanium flow,
and the method row stated per kilogram of titanium. A link to a project homepage
or the equality of two CF values cannot replace that chain.

Every mapping decision needs the following evidence beside its outcome:

| Question | Required trace |
|---|---|
| Where did the assertion come from? | Provider and artifact title, source-list version, original row identifier or file/sheet/row coordinate, retained bytes and digest, retrieval date and rights-aware reference. Keep provider authorship separate from the mapper, contributor and verifier; record unknown names explicitly. |
| What exactly was proposed? | Source and target identities, source and resolved contexts, match relation and direction, original quantity/property units, and every conversion factor with its direction and evidence. Preserve the original statement beside any correction. |
| Why was this decision selected? | The supporting and contradicting assertions, shared ancestry, scope and rationale; the versioned ruling or policy and linked case, issue, discussion or PR. Preserve alternatives and rejected interpretations. |
| Which execution produced it? | Consensus snapshot and build identity, code/ruling revisions, original-input hashes and the generated mapping/factor artifact. A module source link alone cannot establish an executed result. |
| What was checked and accepted? | Actual test or comparison scope, observed result and limitations, independent review/sign-off where recorded, and the exact accepted release. A contributor, intake acknowledgement or passed identity lookup is not a scientific reviewer. |
| Where is it used? | Native source-generation binding, selected method implementation and application policy, consumer release, registered method/inventory and served artifact. Adoption remains unknown until these receiving records exist. |

Store this beside the **individual assertion and resulting decision**, with
stable references to shared artifacts. Do not duplicate a source workbook for
every row or reduce several inherited bridges to a list of independent votes.
Field-level substance and context PROV records and the complete correspondence must
remain available even when a compact export serves downstream consumers.

The current candidate readers authenticate pinned artifacts and require
nonempty evidence references. In particular, the inspected method projector
checks evidence strings and derivation fields; it does **not** independently
dereference and qualify every cited provider row or policy. Its structural
validation therefore cannot be presented as complete captured evidence.
Receiving needs to verify those references against the retained source manifest,
original rows and applicability evidence before that decision becomes accepted.
Keep historical decisions with incomplete captured evidence as historical or held
records; never manufacture missing references, reviewers or dates.

The integrated project still needs a complete, versioned inventory connecting
all of these artifacts and their generations. Curation evidence documents and
statistics can link it; the current case count is not that inventory. The compact
flow export deliberately omits most PROV records and method detail, so it
cannot be the sole custody artifact. Retain the matching rich export/SQLite,
original inputs, complete factor outputs and release receipts.

## Acceptance work before claiming a full replacement

1. **Close source custody and lineage.** Register the recovered inputs, qualify
   versions and bytes, reconcile duplicate/missing source identifiers, and
   preserve every original assertion's disposition. Show source independence
   explicitly; preserve disagreements rather than reconciling by vote count.
2. **Close the native migration census.** Give every consumed native key and
   historical alias a same-generation outcome, including splits, unlinked rows,
   missing bindings and the old biosphere3 versus current ecoinvent differences.
   Source presence is insufficient to certify version coverage.
3. **Receive complete method-policy applications.** Start with the real bounded
   radiation/climate candidates, then extend to all required EF categories and
   retained GLAM, IPCC, ILCD, ecological-scarcity and AWARE treatments. Verify
   region, context, amount basis, sign, uncertainty and once-only conversion.
   Every changed result needs an explanation or remains held.
4. **Compare actual consumers and results.** Use identical pinned inventories
   and provider inputs on the accepted and proposed paths. Retain the complete
   product-by-category population, contributions and missing-factor states;
   compare registration and served use separately. Correctly explained changes
   may improve an old result; reproducing an old defect is not acceptance.
5. **Receive and publish one coherent release.** Record independent scientific
   decisions against exact revisions, preserve standing baseline findings under
   the existing differential gate, and test restoration of the full prior
   bundle. Generate the public claims from that accepted run with named
   denominators. Retire legacy identity authority only for migrated scope.

These are data and acceptance tasks. They do not require another visualisation
layer. The immediate useful result is a traceable migration ledger, followed by
the bounded real-data applications and their numerical comparisons.
